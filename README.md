📊 Retail Sales Analytics

A retail sales data analysis project using Python, Pandas, and Power BI to explore sales performance, identify business trends, and generate actionable insights.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze retail sales data.
- Clean and prepare raw datasets.
- Identify top-performing product categories and regions.
- Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Generate meaningful business insights.

🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Power BI
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel / CSV

- Saved the final dataset as "retail_sales_final.csv".

📈 Power BI Dashboard Features

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Sales
- Profit Margin
- Year-over-Year Growth
- Regional Sales Analysis
- Product Category Analysis
- Monthly Sales Trends
- Payment Method Analysis
- Interactive slicers and filters

💡 Key Business Insights

- Electronics is the highest-performing product category by sales.
- The South region is the highest-performing region.
- The business generated approximately 264.27M in sales and 49.52M in profit.
- The overall profit margin is approximately 18.74%.
- Sales trends and growth can be analyzed across different years.
